About Me

I am a PhD Candidate in the Safavi-Naeini Group at Stanford University. My research focuses on high-performance photonic integrated circuits (PICs) using thin-film lithium niobate (TFLN).

I received my B.S. in Electrical Engineering from KAIST. I have previously worked on deep learning in photonic neural networks and cascaded resonator programmable circuits.

Current research focus

  • Quantum Squeezed Light: Developing sub-threshold optical parametric oscillators for scalable quantum sensing.
  • Nonlinear PICs: High-efficiency second harmonic generation and frequency modulation on thin-film platforms.
